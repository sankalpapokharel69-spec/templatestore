About WebCraft Studio

Great websites shouldn't require a big agency budget.

WebCraft Studio started with a simple frustration: small businesses were paying too much for websites that looked like everyone else's. We believe a restaurant, a salon, or a real-estate agency deserves a website that feels as crafted as the business itself.

So we design and build every template in-house — obsessing over typography, spacing, load speed and accessibility — then hand you clean, commented code you can make your own. No page builders, no bloat, no lock-in.

Today, more than 2,400 businesses in 30+ countries have launched with our templates. Every purchase funds the next one.
