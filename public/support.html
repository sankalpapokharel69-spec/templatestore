Support Center

Answers first — and a real human if you still need one.

How do I receive my template after purchase?

After your payment is verified (usually within a few hours during support hours), a Download button appears on your order page and in My Account → My Orders. The download is a ZIP with all template files.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept eSewa, Khalti and bank transfer. After placing your order, send the total and upload your payment proof (screenshot or receipt) on the confirmation page. We verify manually and unlock your downloads.

Can I use one template on multiple websites?

Each license covers one live website (plus unlimited staging/localhost previews). Need a multi-site license? Contact us — we offer bundle pricing.

Do you offer refunds?

Digital goods can't be "returned", but we stand by our work: if the files are broken or not as described, we fix it or refund you within 7 days of purchase.

Can you customize a template for me?

Yes! We offer customization services starting from $49 depending on scope. Send us a message via the contact form with your requirements for a quote.

Are the templates responsive?

Every template is tested on phones, tablets, laptops and large desktops before it's published. They also include a dark-mode toggle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
